Our Story

About Us

Sprinkle & Bloom began in a tiny kitchen with a big whisk and a bigger imagination. We believe every celebration deserves a baked treat that makes people smile before they even take a bite.

Everything is handmade in small batches with real butter, fresh fruit, and a generous handful of love.

From birthday unicorns to elegant wedding florals, we pour a little joy into every crumb — because life is sweet, and so are you.
